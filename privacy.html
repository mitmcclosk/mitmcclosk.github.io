Hermes EA privacy policy

Hermes EA is a personal assistant program used only by its owner. It is not available to other people.

What Google data it uses

Gmail (read only), Google Calendar, Google Contacts (read only), Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets of the owner's own account, and only after the owner approves access on Google's consent screen.

How the data is used

The data is used only to help the owner manage his own email, calendar, tasks and files. It is processed on the owner's computer and by the AI model services the owner has chosen, only to answer the owner's own requests.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with anyone else, and not used for advertising or to train general AI models.

Storage and deletion

The access token is stored on the owner's computer. The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com, Security, Third-party apps and services.

Limited use

Hermes EA's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
