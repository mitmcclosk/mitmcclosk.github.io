Hermes EA

Hermes EA is a private assistant program used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public and nobody else can sign in to it.

With the owner's permission it reads his Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts and Google Drive files, and it can create calendar events and Drive, Docs and Sheets files for him. It cannot send email.

It runs on the owner's own computer.
